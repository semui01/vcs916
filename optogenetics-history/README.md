# Optogenetics, 1866–2026

A single-file interactive history of optogenetics, from Famintzin's 1866 study of *Chlamydomonas* phototaxis to the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine (Deisseroth, Hegemann, Nagel).

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step; fonts load from Google Fonts.

## Chapters

1. **Timeline**: 64 events in eight research threads, with filters, keyboard stepping and a milestone autoplay.
2. **The race (2004–06)**: lab result vs. publication date for the groups that first put ChR2 into neurons.
3. **Switch lab**: a leaky integrate-and-fire neuron driven by ChR2, Chrimson, ChR1, NpHR, Arch or GtACR1, with a spectrum strip and a fidelity-vs-pulse-rate chart. Teaching model, not fitted to data.
4. **Before the switch**: which design criteria earlier light-control methods met.
5. **Circuits**: schematic mouse brain with 11 optogenetic findings.
6. **Clinic**: vision restoration from blind mice (2006) to a patient (2021).
7. **Credit**: three rules of "invention" and a prize matrix.
